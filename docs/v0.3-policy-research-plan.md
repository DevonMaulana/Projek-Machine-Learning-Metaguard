# MetaGuard v0.3 Policy Research Plan

## Current policy baseline

The repository currently contains only the `government_generic` baseline:

- Perpres No. 39 Tahun 2019 — Satu Data Indonesia.
- Peraturan BPS No. 5 Tahun 2020 — Metadata Statistik.

They support governance-oriented evidence retrieval. They do not establish
healthcare, education, or environment semantic rules by themselves.

## Research categories

Future material must be classified before ingestion:

1. governance policy;
2. sectoral regulation;
3. technical standard;
4. guideline/reference.

Candidate material is classified as `ESSENTIAL`, `SUPPORTING`,
`REFERENCE_ONLY`, or `NOT_SUITABLE`. No candidate regulation is asserted in
this document; external research must use official sources separately.

## Official-source verification checklist

For every candidate, verify:

- official publisher/source and issuing authority;
- title, number, year, document type, and scope;
- current/effective status, amendments, replacement, or revocation;
- relationship to government data governance and the proposed domain;
- whether it supports RAG retrieval, deterministic rule provenance, both, or
  neither;
- whether it is binding policy, technical standard, or guidance only;
- local-file integrity, revision/version information, and retrieval topics.

Do not ingest a policy merely because it mentions a domain. Do not transform a
guideline or reference into a legal requirement.

## Minimum policy metadata model

```text
policy_id, title, policy_pack, domain_id, authority, document_type,
year, effective_status, source, topics, scope, local_file, revision_info
```

Optional metadata may record language, publication date, extraction status, and
a human review note. It must not contain API keys, legal conclusions, or
embedding vectors.

## RAG suitability criteria

A document is suitable for RAG only when it is official or otherwise verified,
locally stored with traceable source metadata, text-extractable without unsafe
OCR assumptions, relevant to defined evidence needs, and granular enough for
chunk/source/page traceability. Duplicate or revised documents require clear
version handling.

## Deterministic-rule suitability criteria

A document supports deterministic provenance only if a human researcher can
translate a stable, testable requirement without unspecified domain judgement.
The future rule must record whether support is policy, technical standard,
invariant, or heuristic. Ambiguous prose belongs to evidence/review context,
not automatic validation.
